About Me

I'm Bao An, a young pharmacist who graduated from VNU University of Medicine and Pharmacy, Vietnam National University, Hanoi, and I currently work in Quality Assurance (QA) at Tasco Pharma. I value precision and attention to detail – qualities that both QA work and the chessboard demand. I still enter tournaments from time to time, most recently the VCC Open Chess Tournament 2026.

In my free time, I grab my backpack to hike up mountains for the flowers, trek through forests in search of waterfalls, or head to the beach for some sea breeze – always making sure to capture the beautiful moments along the way. To keep up with all these adventures, I train at the gym regularly.

I'm a true cat person, and I treasure every moment spent with my family and friends. My goal is to contribute, in my own way, to bringing safe, high-quality medicines to people.
